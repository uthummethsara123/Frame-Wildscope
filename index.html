About Frame 2026

Where Visual Art Meets Storytelling

Organized by the Sivali Central College Media Unit (**SCCMU**), **FRAME** is Sri Lanka’s premier annual photographic festival built to empower student photographers and visual artists.

Creative Vision

Providing a creative platform for young photographers to showcase perspective, technical skill, and artistic lighting across multiple themes.

4 Photo Categories

Competing in Colour, Monochrome, Nature & Wildlife, and Street & Travel photography, judged by industry professionals.

Wildscope Marathon

An exclusive live wildlife photo marathon inside the UNESCO World Heritage **Sinharaja Rainforest**.

Islandwide Scope

Connecting schools and open participants across the island under Inter-School, Inner-School, and Open competition categories.
